What I do

How I work on a product

01

Understand how customers buy

  • In-depth customer interviews: discussion guide, recordings, takeaways
  • Competitive analysis and a market positioning map
  • Segments, personas and the real buying criteria
  • Barriers: why people look but don’t buy

In practice. I tested demand for a market analytics platform: 31 sign-ups, 23 interviews. It turned out customers wanted conclusions they could act on, data alone wasn’t enough, so we rebuilt the offer.

02

Shape the offer

  • Positioning and key messages for each segment
  • Product line, product requirements, assortment
  • Product economics and cost control
  • Naming, visual identity and agency briefs

In practice. I found that exporters pick a representative who takes the risk off their hands. That became the promise “protect it from risks”, plus a new company name that reads in any language.

03

Take it to market

  • Launch plan and channel mix
  • Website, SEO and AI search, organic LinkedIn
  • Trade shows, business programs, special projects
  • Partnerships, co-marketing and barter deals, ad budgets up to ~$36K

In practice. With no ad budget, through LinkedIn and SEO alone, we went from zero to 15 inbound leads a month from international companies in 4 months.

04

Help sales close

  • Sales arguments and enablement materials
  • Lead qualification and a CRM where no lead slips through
  • Account management and renewals
  • Reporting on what brings in leads and what doesn’t

In practice. I qualify every inbound lead on volume and fit and join the CEO’s client meetings. The supplier base grew from 5 to 12.

Case studies

Challenge, approach, results

01 · B2B · international trade

Building marketing from scratch for a company that ran on word of mouth for 8 years

Challenge. The agency represents international fresh produce suppliers in Russian retail. It had zero inbound leads, fuzzy positioning, and client records scattered across spreadsheets.

What I did. Figured out why clients chose the company: they wanted someone on the ground who takes the risk off their hands. Built the positioning, a new name and a website measured by lead conversion around that. Set up the CRM, LinkedIn and SEO.

  • 0 → 15inbound leads a month within 4 months
  • 5 → 12suppliers in the portfolio
  • $0ad spend
02 · B2B · market analytics

Testing demand for a market analytics platform

Challenge. The company wanted a landing page for its market data platform. I reframed the brief: is there a business model here, and who is it for?

What I did. Two access tiers: free in exchange for a 15-minute call that doubled as an interview, and paid at $300 a year to test willingness to pay. For the platform itself, I picked and prepared product analytics: events, funnels, retention.

  • 31access requests
  • 23interviews with potential customers
  • 74%request-to-call conversion
03 · B2B · website and search

A website international clients can actually find

Challenge. Every client is abroad, yet the 2018 website loaded poorly outside Russia and barely showed up on Google: only 2 of 14 key queries ranked in the top 10.

What I did. Audited access, tech and SEO. Built an English keyword map: 38 clusters and 14 article topics. Shipped quick fixes on the live site and a new navigation built around exporters’ tasks. The new homepage prototype is approved.

  • 117pages: one H1 each, sitemap, redirects
  • 38keyword clusters in the search map
  • 2 of 14queries in the top 10: the baseline
